# Mix & Match: Constructor — Civilization VI Mod

**Mix & Match: Constructor** gives you complete freedom to build your own custom civilization and leader directly from the game setup lobby.

Ever wanted to build Roman Legions while playing as Germany? Combine Peter the Great's Cossacks with Gilgamesh's war carts and Pericles' Culture bonuses? With **Mix & Match: Constructor**, you can assemble your dream civilization with any combination of traits, units, infrastructure, and leader identities.

## Features

* **Civilization Ability:** Choose any civilization unique trait from any civilization available in your game.
* **Leader Ability:** Pick any leader trait to dictate your strategic bonuses.
* **Unique Unit:** Deploy any civilization's signature unique unit.
* **Unique Infrastructure:** Construct any unique district, building, or tile improvement.
* **Cosmetic Identity:** Choose how your leader looks on the diplomacy stage and on the victory/defeat screen.
* **Mystery & Random Modes:** Pick random abilities or play with hidden Mystery traits for endless replayability.
* **Full Multiplayer Support:** Fully functional in Singleplayer, Multiplayer lobbies, and Hotseat modes.
* **All DLCs Supported:** Works seamlessly with Base Game, Rise and Fall, Gathering Storm, New Frontier Pass, and Leader Pass. Available choices dynamically adapt based on the DLCs installed.

## How to Play

1. Start a new game and head to the **Create Game / Advanced Setup** screen.
2. Select the **Mix & Match: Constructor** leader.
3. Click the Constructor icons to choose your custom loadout:
   * Civilization Trait
   * Leader Trait
   * Unique Unit
   * Unique Infrastructure (District / Improvement / Building)
   * Cosmetic Leader Appearance
4. Launch the match and dominate the world with your unique synergy!

## Installation

### Manual Installation (GitHub / Nexus / CivFanatics)
1. Download the mod archive.
2. Extract the folder into your Civilization VI mods directory:
   ```text
   Documents\My Games\Sid Meier's Civilization VI\Mods\MixAndMatch
   ```
3. Launch Civilization VI, open **Additional Content -> Mods**, and ensure **Mix & Match: Constructor** is enabled.
