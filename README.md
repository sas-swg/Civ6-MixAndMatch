# Mix & Match: Constructor for Civilization VI

**Mix & Match: Constructor** allows players to create customized civilizations by combining abilities, units, infrastructure, and leader identities directly from the game setup lobby.

## Links
* CivFanatics: https://forums.civfanatics.com/resources/mix-match-constructor.32948/
* Steam Workshop: https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3814730306

## Features
* **Civilization Ability:** Choose any civilization unique ability from any civilization you own.
* **Leader Ability:** Pick any leader trait to guide your empire.
* **Unique Unit:** Deploy any civilization's signature unique unit.
* **Unique Infrastructure:** Construct any unique district, building, or tile improvement.
* **Cosmetic Identity:** Choose how your leader looks diplomatically and on victory/defeat screens.
* **Mystery & Random Modes:** Pick random abilities or play with hidden Mystery traits for endless replayability.
* **Game Modes:** Fully functional in Singleplayer, Multiplayer lobbies, and Hotseat.
* **DLC Support:** Compatible with Base Game, Rise and Fall, Gathering Storm, New Frontier Pass, and Leader Pass.

## Installation
1. Download the mod files or repository archive.
2. Place the `MixAndMatch` folder into your Civilization VI mods directory:
   `Documents\My Games\Sid Meier's Civilization VI\Mods\MixAndMatch`
3. Launch Civilization VI, ensure **Mix & Match: Constructor** is enabled under **Additional Content -> Mods**, and start a new game!
